\# Threat Intelligence Basics



\## Overview



Threat intelligence is the collection, analysis, and sharing of information related to cyber threats, attackers, vulnerabilities, and malicious activity.



Organizations use threat intelligence to:

\- improve threat detection

\- strengthen defenses

\- support incident response

\- understand attacker behavior



Threat intelligence is widely used in SOC operations and threat hunting.



\---



\## Types of Threat Intelligence



\### Strategic Intelligence



High-level information about:

\- threat trends

\- industry risks

\- attacker motivations



Used by:

\- executives

\- leadership teams



\---



\### Tactical Intelligence



Focuses on attacker techniques and behavior.



Examples:

\- phishing campaigns

\- malware techniques

\- attack patterns



\---



\### Operational Intelligence



Information about active threats and ongoing attacks.



Examples:

\- attacker infrastructure

\- malicious campaigns

\- threat actor activity



\---



\### Technical Intelligence



Technical indicators used for detection.



Examples:

\- malicious IP addresses

\- file hashes

\- suspicious domains

\- URLs



\---



\## Indicators of Compromise (IOCs)



IOCs are artifacts associated with malicious activity.



Examples:

\- malware hashes

\- phishing domains

\- command and control IPs

\- malicious URLs



\---



\## Threat Intelligence Sources



Examples:

\- open-source intelligence (OSINT)

\- commercial feeds

\- government advisories

\- security vendors

\- internal investigations



\---



\## Common Threat Intelligence Platforms



Examples:

\- MISP

\- VirusTotal

\- AlienVault OTX

\- Recorded Future



\---



\## Threat Intelligence in SOC Operations



SOC analysts use threat intelligence to:

\- enrich alerts

\- identify malicious infrastructure

\- prioritize investigations

\- improve detection rules



\---



\## Threat Hunting with Intelligence



Threat hunters search environments for:

\- known malicious IPs

\- suspicious domains

\- attacker behaviors

\- malware indicators



\---



\## Threat Actor Profiling



Threat intelligence may include:

\- attacker motivations

\- target industries

\- attack methods

\- geographic activity



\---



\## Malware Intelligence



Analysts study:

\- malware families

\- command and control servers

\- persistence mechanisms

\- delivery methods



\---



\## Threat Intelligence Sharing



Organizations often share intelligence to improve collective defense.



Benefits:

\- faster detection

\- improved awareness

\- stronger collaboration



\---



\## Intelligence Challenges



Common challenges:

\- false positives

\- outdated indicators

\- large data volumes

\- attribution difficulties



\---



\## Security Best Practices



Recommended practices:

\- validate intelligence sources

\- automate IOC ingestion

\- correlate intelligence with SIEM alerts

\- monitor emerging threats continuously



\---



\## Importance in Cybersecurity



Threat intelligence improves:

\- threat detection

\- SOC visibility

\- incident response

\- proactive defense strategies



\---



\## Conclusion



Threat intelligence helps organizations understand and respond to cyber threats more effectively. Continuous intelligence collection and analysis strengthen security operations and improve threat detection capabilities.
