\# SOC Basics



\## Overview



A Security Operations Center (SOC) is a centralized team responsible for monitoring, detecting, analyzing, and responding to cybersecurity incidents within an organization.



SOC analysts work with logs, alerts, threat intelligence, and security tools to identify suspicious activity and reduce security risks.



\---



\## Main Responsibilities of a SOC



Common SOC responsibilities include:



\- Monitoring security alerts

\- Investigating suspicious activity

\- Detecting threats and attacks

\- Incident response

\- Log analysis

\- Threat hunting

\- Escalating critical incidents



\---



\## SOC Team Structure



\### Tier 1 Analyst



Tier 1 analysts are responsible for:

\- monitoring alerts

\- triaging incidents

\- identifying false positives

\- escalating serious threats



\---



\### Tier 2 Analyst



Tier 2 analysts:

\- perform deeper investigations

\- analyze malware

\- investigate attacker behavior

\- support incident response



\---



\### Tier 3 Analyst



Tier 3 analysts focus on:

\- threat hunting

\- advanced analysis

\- developing detection rules

\- improving SOC operations



\---



\## Common SOC Tools



\### SIEM (Security Information and Event Management)



SIEM tools collect and analyze logs from multiple systems.



Popular SIEM platforms:

\- Splunk

\- IBM QRadar

\- Microsoft Sentinel

\- Elastic SIEM



\---



\### EDR (Endpoint Detection and Response)



EDR tools monitor endpoint activity and detect malicious behavior.



Examples:

\- CrowdStrike

\- Microsoft Defender

\- SentinelOne



\---



\## Alert Triage Process



SOC analysts typically:

1\. Receive an alert

2\. Validate the alert

3\. Investigate indicators

4\. Assess severity

5\. Escalate or close the case



\---



\## Common Indicators of Compromise (IOCs)



Examples of suspicious indicators:

\- failed login attempts

\- unusual outbound traffic

\- malware detections

\- privilege escalation

\- suspicious PowerShell execution



\---



\## Importance of Log Analysis



SOC teams analyze logs from:

\- firewalls

\- servers

\- endpoints

\- Active Directory

\- cloud environments



Logs help identify:

\- unauthorized access

\- brute force attacks

\- malware activity

\- insider threats



\---



\## Incident Response in SOC



SOC teams play a major role in:

\- identifying incidents

\- containing attacks

\- preserving evidence

\- supporting recovery



\---



\## Threat Intelligence



Threat intelligence provides information about:

\- attacker techniques

\- malicious IP addresses

\- malware indicators

\- emerging vulnerabilities



\---



\## Conclusion



SOC operations are a critical part of modern cybersecurity defense. Understanding SOC workflows, tools, and analyst responsibilities is important for anyone pursuing a career in cybersecurity.
