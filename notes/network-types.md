Network Types: LAN, WAN, and MAN

Date - February 23, 2026

Objective - To understand different types of networks based on geographical scope and their typical use cases.

LAN (Local Area Network)
- Covers a small geographic area such as a home, office, or campus
- High data transfer speeds
- Privately owned and managed
- Examples: Home Wi-Fi, office networks

MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)
- Covers a city or large campus area
- Larger than LAN but smaller than WAN
- Often managed by organizations or service providers
- Examples: City-wide networks, university networks across a city

WAN (Wide Area Network)
- Covers a large geographic area (country or global)
- Connects multiple LANs and MANs
- Typically managed by ISPs or telecom providers
- Example: The Internet

Key Differences
- LAN: Small area, high speed
- MAN: City-scale coverage
- WAN: Large area, comparatively higher latency

Security Relevance
- LANs are vulnerable to internal threats
- MANs and WANs rely heavily on secure routing and encryption
- WAN security is critical due to public infrastructure usage

Reflection - Understanding network types helps in designing secure network architectures and selecting appropriate security controls.
